What it does

Search every program

Admission averages, required Grade 12 courses, co-op, tuition, OUAC codes, and whether a supplementary application is waiting for you — for all 1,406 of them, in one search field.

See where you stand

Enter your top six marks once and every program sorts into Reach, Target or Safety against your real average. Until you enter them it shows the tier as unknown rather than estimating one.

Never miss a deadline

A live countdown to the next OUAC date, and a tracker that follows each application from shortlisted through applied, supplementary sent, and offer.

Why the numbers hold

Most admission tools hand you a confident percentage no matter what you've told them. That is worse than useless when you're choosing where to spend four years — once a made-up number is on the screen it is indistinguishable from a real one.

oHub refuses to do that. Until you enter your marks it will not estimate them, and where a university has published no cutoff it says as much rather than inventing one. Every figure traces back to something you entered or something a university published, and each program links out to the official page, because averages and deadlines change.

Your marks stay yours

oHub asks for your name, school, OUAC reference and grades. That is sensitive, so it is built so none of it can reach us — there is no server for it to reach.

  • No account, ever. No sign-up, no email, no password to forget.
  • Stored on your device only. Everything lives in your own browser. There is no oHub database.
  • No tracking, no analytics. Nobody is counting your clicks or selling your data.
  • Your OUAC reference is masked on every screen except the one where you edit it.
  • Erase it all in one tap. Settings has a delete control that actually deletes.

Start where you are.

Open it, search a program you're curious about, add your marks whenever you feel like it. Nothing is required to get something out of it.
