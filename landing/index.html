How it works

Three things, done properly.

Applying to university in Ontario means juggling OUAC, a dozen program pages and a supplementary form you found out about last week. oHub puts the parts that matter in one place.

📚

Search every program

All 1,406 undergraduate programs across all 23 Ontario universities — admission averages, required Grade 12 courses, co-op, tuition, OUAC codes and whether a supplementary application is waiting for you.

🎯

See where you stand

Enter your top six marks once and every program sorts into Reach, Target or Safety against your real average. Haven't entered marks? It says so, instead of guessing.

🗓️

Never miss a deadline

A live countdown to the next OUAC date, plus a tracker that follows each application from shortlisted through applied, supplementary sent, and offer.

Why you can trust the numbers

It will tell you when it doesn't know.

Most admission tools will hand you a confident percentage no matter what you've told them. That is worse than useless when you are choosing where to spend the next four years — a made-up number is indistinguishable from a real one once it's on screen.

oHub refuses to do that. If you haven't entered your marks, it doesn't estimate them. If a university hasn't published a cutoff, it says so rather than inventing one. Every figure you see traces back to something you entered or something a university published — and each program links out to the official page, because averages and deadlines change.

Privacy

Your marks are nobody else's business.

oHub asks for your name, school, OUAC reference and grades. That is sensitive, so the app is built so that none of it can reach us — there is no server for it to reach.

  • ✓No account, ever. No sign-up, no email, no password to forget.
  • ✓Stored on your device only. Everything lives in your own browser. There is no oHub database.
  • ✓No tracking or analytics. Nobody is counting your clicks or selling your data.
  • ✓Your OUAC reference is masked on every screen except the one where you edit it.
  • ✓Erase it all in one tap. Settings has a delete control that actually deletes.
